# Global Procurement & Supplier Insolvency Risk Policy

## Section 1: Insolvency and Default Events
In the event of insolvency, bankruptcy, or failure of a Tier-1 supplier, the Procurement Risk Committee must immediately compute total open balances owed to the supplier and trigger switchover protocols to approved alternate suppliers within 14 days.

## Section 2: Financial Exposure Thresholds
Financial exposure exceeding EUR 100,000.00 requires executive escalation and mandate activation of secondary source allocation. Any outstanding invoices owed to the supplier must be frozen until audit reconciliation is completed.

## Section 3: Alternate Source Contingency
Contracts with primary suppliers must maintain at least one dual-source clause guaranteeing 50% capacity allocation to designated alternate suppliers during supply disruptions.
