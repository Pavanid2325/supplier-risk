# Master Supply Agreement for SUP-40217

## Section 1: Scope of Supply
Apex Microelectronics GmbH (SUP-40217) is designated as the sole-source supplier for specialized micro-controllers under Project SagaEngine.

## Section 2: Liability and Insolvency Provisions
If SUP-40217 initiates insolvency proceedings or experiences operational failure:
1. Buyer reserves the right to terminate all pending purchase orders immediately.
2. Buyer may offset any prepayments or unfulfilled order balances against outstanding accounts payable.
3. The Buyer shall transition micro-controller orders to alternate suppliers Nordic SemiTech AB (SUP-62001) or Vanguard Micro-Components (SUP-73012).

## Section 3: Termination Penalties
Early termination for cause due to supplier financial failure shall incur zero early termination fees. All intellectual property tooling remains the sole property of Buyer.
